## What is this study?
We are testing whether an AI interview-practice tool gives scores that agree with trained human evaluators and treat
different candidates fairly. You will answer about 3-8 interview questions (typed or spoken) and receive practice feedback.
It takes about 20 minutes.

## What we collect
- Your answers (text, and a voice recording if you choose spoken mode)
- Your first language, region and (optionally) gender, used only to check whether scoring is fair across groups
- Optional usability questionnaire answers (SUS)

We do **not** collect your name. You get a random ID (for example `P_1a2b3c4d`).

## How it is used
- Human raters read your answer without seeing who you are, and score it. The AI also scores it.
- Results are reported only as group statistics. No individual is identified.
- Voice recordings are used only to create a corrected transcript for checking speech-to-text accuracy. Recordings are not published.

## Your rights
- Taking part is **voluntary**. You may skip any question or stop at any time.
- You can delete all your data at any time using **Delete My Data** in the app, or by asking the team with your participant ID.
- The score is **practice feedback only**. It is not a judgement of you and is never used for placement, grades or hiring.

## Data storage
Data are stored on [laptop / college server], accessible only to the project team and the assigned raters. Raw answers and audio
are not uploaded to public repositories. Data will be deleted after [date / project evaluation].

## Consent
By ticking the consent box in the app you confirm that you have read this information, you are 18 or older, and you agree to take part.

Questions? Contact [name, email].
