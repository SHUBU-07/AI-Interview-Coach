# Rater Guide: AI Interview Coach Study

Thank you for helping. Your independent judgement is the benchmark the AI is compared against, so how you rate matters more than anything else in this study.

## Your task
Read each anonymous answer and score four dimensions from 1 to 5 using the written level descriptions in `rubric.yaml`
(shown in the app under "View Anchors"):

1. Relevance and correctness
2. Depth and completeness
3. Structure and clarity (STAR for behavioural questions)
4. Language use (**do not penalise accent, dialect or non-native grammar** if the meaning is clear)

You rate the **content of the written transcript**. Do not guess how it sounded.

## Rules
- Use your **own unique Rater ID**. Never share it.
- Rate **independently**. Do not discuss answers or scores with other raters until the whole study is finished.
- You cannot see other raters' scores or the AI's scores. Please do not look for them.
- Score every dimension. Use the whole 1-5 range when the answer deserves it.
- Add a short evidence note (a quote that justified your score) when you can.
- Take a break after about 50 answers. Tired ratings are noisy.
- Do not try to work out who wrote an answer. If you recognise someone, tell the team and skip it.

## Calibration session (about 1 hour, before real rating)
1. The team explains the rubric and walks through the two training examples per dimension in `rubric.yaml`.
2. Everyone scores the same 10 practice answers independently (use `data/demo/` answers or answers the team writes; do **not** reuse real participant answers).
3. Compare scores and discuss each disagreement of 2 or more points. Agree on how to read the descriptors.
4. Rate a second set of 5 practice answers and check that you now agree more closely.
5. Then start the real rating. After this point, no more discussion.

## Workload
About 2 minutes per answer. For 150 answers: about 5 hours per rater, spread over several days.

## Feedback audit (optional second task)
After you have rated an answer, you can open **Feedback & SUS > Rater** and judge the AI's feedback: tick each claim that is truly
supported by the answer, and rate how actionable the advice is. Do this only for answers you have already rated.
